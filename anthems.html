School Anthems & Cultural Values

Building national pride, moral values, and civic responsibility in our little stars from an early age.

National School's Anthem

CHORUS

We young women and men of Uganda are matching along the path of Education singing and dancing with Joy together uniting for a better Uganda.

STANZA I

We are the pillars of tomorrow's Uganda let's rise now embrace true knowledge yielding discipline resourcefulness to rebuild a great peal.

STANZA II

We know the way into the land of enlightenment as thorns, creepers, valleys and mountains come what may we shall overcome for the glorious times to come.

STANZA III

Parents and teachers and the youth of this nation rise with us, support our endeavers led by God who is the source of life to up lift our motherland.

Uganda National Anthem

1. OH UGANDA

Oh Uganda may God uphold thee
We lay our future in thy hands
United free for liberty
Together we'll always stand.

2. LAND OF FREEDOM

Oh Uganda, The land of freedom
Our love and labour we give.
And with neighbors all, at our country's call
In peace and friendship we'll live

3. PEARL OF AFRICA

Oh Uganda!
The land that feeds us,
By sun and fertile soil grown,
For our own dear land
We'll always stand,
The pearl of Africa's Crown
