Our Programs

Nurturing focused minds and bright futures through structured learning and play.

Early Childhood Education & Care

We provide a safe, engaging environment tailored to different stages of your child's early development.

Day Care (1 – 3 Years)

A warm, home-like environment for our youngest stars. We focus on sensory play, basic motor skills, daily hygiene habits, and providing the utmost care and personal attention while you are at work.

View Requirements

Nursery School (3 – 6 Years)

Introducing the fundamentals of learning through structured Baby, Middle, and Top classes. Children engage in our reading sessions, basic math concepts, and social group activities with dedicated teachers.

View Rates

Primary Section (5 – 6 Years)

Preparing pupils for primary education (P1 to P4). We emphasize literacy, numeracy, critical thinking, and character building to ensure a smooth transition to full primary schooling.

View Pricing

Beyond the Classroom

Education at Little Stars is holistic. We believe talent growth is a must!

  • Swimming: Fridays and weekends are reserved for fun water sessions to build physical health and confidence.
  • Concerts & Cultural Days: High-priority annual celebrations dedicated to talent discovery in music, dance, and drama.
  • Sports Days: Exciting physical games where pupils and parents mingle in fun competition.
  • Health & Hygiene: Teaching essential health habits such as daily tooth brushing and personal care.
