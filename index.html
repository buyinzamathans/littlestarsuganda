Our Campus & Culture

Nurturing minds, building character, and empowering bright futures in Nansana.

Safe & Vibrant Environment

Our colorful, clean compound and playground facilities ensure learning and play happen in a secure, fun-filled setting.

Moral & Character Building

We instil life long principles: discipline, respect, cleanliness, and faith, shaping well-rounded children for tomorrow.

Academic Excellence

From Early Childhood Development through Kindergarten, our disciplined classrooms foster curiosity and early literacy.

Our Co-Curricular Activities Are Beyond Fun!

Talent growth is a must. Discover what makes learning fun at Little Stars.

Swim Time Let's Go!

Cause Fridays and weekends are for taking a dip! Our swimming sessions are a fantastic way for children to stay active, build confidence in the water, and have a blast together.

Concerts & Cultural Days

Today is the day of joy and celebration! One of the best things that can happen to your child is the discovery of their talent. At Little Stars, this is a high priority.

Sports Day

Sports Day at Little Stars is always a blast! It's a wonderful opportunity for our little ones to mingle in fun physical activities and for parents to join in on the action.

Reading Class

Mondays are beautiful here at our school with the best teachers. Never a dull day in learning! We foster a love for reading and curiosity in every child.

Healthy Smiles, Bright Futures

We mean it when we say we care as you do! Teaching our little stars essential daily habits like brushing their teeth is just part of our fun, daily routine.
