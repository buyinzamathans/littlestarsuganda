Character Building

Moral & Environmental Guidance

Our green trees and gardens are mounted with positive character-shaping signboards that inspire children and reinforce our core values daily.

Structured Learning Space

Classroom Doors & Social Discipline

Each classroom block is distinctively named and labeled (such as K1 Pelican and K2 Swan). Prominent entry signs reinforce discipline and social behavior expectations.

"Greeting is a sign of discipline"

Reminding young learners to cultivate respect, gratitude, and warm community interactions with peers and elders.

"Education is the best opportunity"

Instilling a lifelong valuation for curiosity, listening skills, and academic diligence.

Facility Architecture

Our classroom verandas open into neat garden borders and shaded walkways. We maintain strict hygiene standards and ensure excellent natural lighting in all learning spaces.
